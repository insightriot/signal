# `M6.E7` — per-unit calibration

**FEATURE, inside a FULL project.** The fourth use of the per-unit dial, after `M6.E4`, `M6.E5` and
`M6.E6`.

**Derived from the ordered gates, not assigned** (`references/tier-definitions.md`, *"first match
wins"*). No FULL escalator fires — `stakes` is `major` not `catastrophic`, `reversibility` is
`moderate` not `irreversible`, `horizon` is `months` not `years`. SPIKE fails on `stakes: major`.
SKETCH fails on `scope: feature`. FEATURE is the default and the answer.

**Why `stakes: major` rather than `minor`.** The advisor's output is read to decide what gets built
next, and `BACKLOG.md`'s row sequenced this Epic behind `M5.E10` precisely because *"an advisor that
emits unverified claims is the claim-integrity defect with a wider blast radius."* Not
`catastrophic`: it changes nothing on its own (`D-M6E7-4`), so a person is always between the claim
and the consequence.

**Why `novelty: rare` rather than `first-for-org`.** The shape is well known — Signal has a
known-good output to reproduce and two installed tools implementing the pattern. What is new is the
instance, not the idea. Calling it `first-for-org` would have routed it to SPIKE on the second gate,
which is wrong: this is not exploratory work, the answer is already known.

## The one override, and why it is not tier inflation

**`nyquist_enforcement: strict`** — FEATURE's default is `basic`.

Citation-resolution is the property this Epic exists to guarantee: every claim the advisor emits
must name a path that resolves. `basic` samples coverage; a sampled guarantee is not one. This is the
narrow, testable place where the claim-integrity risk lands.

⚠ **Everything else stays at FEATURE deliberately.** The temptation was to raise the tier because the
subject matter is claim integrity, and `D-M6E7-3` records why that was refused: **a dial turned up
out of unease is not a check.** The risk is handled by an acceptance criterion that mechanically
resolves every citation — which either passes or fails — rather than by rigor the tier cannot aim.

**Escalation trigger, stated in advance:** if the advisor is given any ability to *write* to
`BACKLOG.md`, or to select rather than propose, `D-M6E7-4`'s two limits are gone and this
recalibrates — `reversibility` and `stakes` both move.
