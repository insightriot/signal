# M5.E8 — Plan: the measurement foundation

**Epic:** M5.E8 · **Tier:** FULL (strict gates, TDD required, Nyquist strict)
**Spec:** [`M5.E8-REQUIREMENTS.md`](M5.E8-REQUIREMENTS.md) · **Research:** [`M5.E8-RESEARCH.md`](M5.E8-RESEARCH.md)
**Runtime:** Node 22+, **no new dependencies** (the harness shells out to `claude -p`).

---

## Phase goal

Produce a harness that answers *"does this instruction change what an agent does?"* for one real
instruction — **and publish the honest fraction of Signal's corpus that question can ever reach.**

---

## What research changed

**R1 computed FR5's ceiling before any code: ~11% trace-measurable, ~89% not.** That is now a
**Slice 1 deliverable rather than a Slice 4 report**, because it determines whether the harness is
worth building at all — and because a number produced *before* the harness exists cannot be
accused of being fitted to it.

**R4 cut the canary set from five instructions to one.** Two arms × N runs means 6 invocations per
instruction at N=3. Proving the method on one subject costs 6 runs; opening with five costs 30
before anyone knows the method works.

## Slices

### Slice 1 — The ceiling, published first (FR5) · **S**

| Task | What | AC |
|---|---|---|
| **S1.t1** | Classifier over `commands/*.md`: directive lines → trace-measurable / not, with the split rule written down and testable. | AC5.1 |

### Slice 2 — The harness skeleton (FR1) · **M**

| Task | What | AC |

**Depends on:** nothing (parallel with S1). **Complexity: M.**

### Slice 3 — The control arm (FR2, FR3) · **L**

| Task | What | AC |

## The risk this plan is most exposed to

**The harness may show the instruction does nothing.** If `B41`'s phase-entry rule shows no
difference between arms, Signal will have spent an Epic proving one of its own shipped fixes is
inert.

**That is the correct outcome to want.** A measurement that can only confirm is not a measurement,
and NFR4 already names the honest null as a first-class result. It is written here so nobody
rediscovers it as a crisis mid-EXECUTE and starts tuning the harness until the answer improves.
