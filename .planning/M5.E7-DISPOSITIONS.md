## §2 — The organizing principle, and it is not a preference

**Everything prompt-shaped is `continue` until Signal can measure whether a prompt intervention
fires.** This is not caution; it is D-M5E7-12 applied consistently.

`ce-retune`'s A/A noise floor measured **7-of-12 workflow adherence and a 7.12× output-token spread
on byte-identical code**. Signal has **no test anywhere that asserts a prompt instruction was
obeyed** (verified by grep, `M5.E7-COUNTERFACTUAL.md` §3.2). Together those mean: **port a
prompt-shaped capability today and you cannot tell afterwards whether it did anything.** Most of the
25 checkboxes in `MILESTONE-5.md` are prompt-shaped. Committing build capacity to them now buys
unfalsifiable work.

The same logic makes the exception clear. **Deterministic, file-shaped capabilities are `build`-able
now** — a check either runs or it doesn't, and the suite proves which. That line, not enthusiasm,
separates the `build` column from the `continue` column below.
