## S1.t3 — the contract question the PLAN does not answer, and how it resolved

The plan assumes AC1.5's *"artifact pair"* exists as a pair. **On disk it does not.**
`eval-project-C` has no phase-scoped REQUIREMENTS artifact: its requirements live in a
**project-scoped** `REQUIREMENTS.md` (713 lines, every phase), while VERIFICATION is
**phase-scoped**. Diffed naively, every id from every other phase reports `missing` — a red wall that
says nothing about Phase 11, and a completeness claim written from the shape of the work rather than
the artifact.

Resolved as `D-M5E10-6` + **AC1.6** / **AC1.7**. Two things are worth carrying forward about *how* it
resolved, because neither was reasoning:

**The first rule I wrote was wrong, and measuring it is what said so.** `D-M5E10-6` originally scoped
the denominator to *"sections whose heading or body names the unit."* Run against the file: `NFR-10`
is a **different unit's** section that mentions this one in its body and would have contributed
**70+ foreign ids**, while `FR-16` — the main group — is not named by its own heading at all. Neither
half survived. **ID-group correspondence** ships instead: the denominator is the union of the
requirement groups the VERIFICATION itself cites, so no prose is read and no prose can be misread.
The decision was **retracted in place**, not annotated.

**Both halves of AC1.5 exist in the field; neither needed authoring.** ⚠ This log previously said the
passing half *"will be an amendment we author."* **That was wrong.** The source project's git history
carries both revisions: the pre-amendment VERIFICATION cites `NFR-9.1` / `NFR-9.3`, and the
post-amendment one adds `NFR-9.2` — the real omission, and the real fix. Both are frozen in
`tests/fixtures/claim-integrity/`.

That also cleared a risk worth stating: because the pre-amendment copy already cited two `NFR-9` ids,
the group was in scope, so group-correspondence **catches the actual field defect**. Had the whole
group been absent, the check would have called the real defect out-of-scope — the blind spot AC1.6
now reports by count instead of passing over.
