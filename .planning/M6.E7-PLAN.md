# `M6.E7` — Plan

**Goal:** a read-only `/sig:advise` that reads this project's own `.planning/` corpus and emits a
dated advisory artifact recommending what to work on next — **where every claim it makes carries a
citation that mechanically resolves.**

**Tier FEATURE** ([`M6.E7-PROFILE.md`](M6.E7-PROFILE.md)) → `plan_validation_dims: core`,
`research_parallelism: 2` (run), `nyquist_enforcement: strict`.

## What research changed about the plan

Two parallel passes ran. Both moved the design; neither confirmed it.

1. **`parseBacklogRows` defaults to `maxDepth: 3`, and Signal's own promoted rows sit at `####`.** A
   default-depth read returns **zero** of them. The module says so in its own source: *"a READER
   that must not mistake 'nested deeper' for 'no work' passes `maxDepth: 4`."* The advisor is
   exactly that reader. **This is the single most likely way this Epic ships something confidently
   blind** — it is `B94`'s class, and it would look like a working command.
2. **Nothing in this repo resolves a `path:line` citation.** Four link scanners exist
   (`checkInternalLinks`, `checkOrphanDocs`, `checkDanglingReferences`, `scanDanglingLinks`); all are
   `.md`-only, inline-`](…)`-only, and **none checks a line number**. `checkDanglingReferences` is
   about *identifiers*, not paths. `doc-hygiene.js` also carries an explicit contract that its scope
   defaults must not change. **So the verifier is new code, and it is the Epic's load-bearing
   property.**
3. **`readState` THROWS** (`StateSchemaError`) on a missing/unknown `schema_version`. Uncaught, that
   is a crash where FR7 promises a `cannot-check` line.
4. **`walkBugEntries` returns no line number**, so `BUGS.md` citations cannot be `path:line` today.
5. **Two mutually-blind milestone Epic-row parsers exist** — filed as a bug this session, verified by
   running both. Bears directly on FR1's "what is open or closed".

## Slices

Three, each independently verifiable. **S1 first because it is the property everything else claims.**

### S1 — The citation verifier (`plugin/tools/lib/citations.js`)

Pure, no IO except `existsSync` + a line count. No dependency on any other slice.

> ### ⚠ The advisor's grammar is NARROWER than the frozen review's, deliberately — and that is a decision, not an oversight
>
> The first draft of this slice claimed to recognise *"the grammar the known-good artifact actually
> uses."* **It did not.** Enumerated over
> [`BACKLOG-REVIEW-2026-08-09.md`](BACKLOG-REVIEW-2026-08-09.md): `path` ×68, `path:line` ×35,
> **bare-line `:219` ×32**, range/list ×8. The bare-line form — where the path is inherited from the
> surrounding sentence — is the **second-largest class** and the draft missed it entirely. That is the
> failure the task's own warning was written about, one form over.
>
> **Resolution: the advisor emits repo-root-relative `path` or `path:line` only.** Bare-line and
> comma-list forms are *not emitted*, so they need not be parsed. This is legitimate because the
> verifier checks **the advisor's own output**, not arbitrary documents — but it means the extractor
> **cannot be pointed at the frozen review** and must not be described as if it could.
>
> ⚠ **Paths must be repo-root-relative, and the frozen review's are not.** 56 of ~65 of its
> path-bearing citations do not resolve from repo root — `MILESTONE-5.md` is `.planning/`-relative,
> `SIGNAL-V2-ROADMAP.md` is `analysis/`-relative, `plan.md` is `plugin/commands/`-relative. **The
> renderer enforces repo-root; `verifyCitations` does no root-guessing.** One convention, checked,
> rather than a search path that makes a wrong citation look right.

- **t1.1** `extractCitations(text)` → `[{raw, path, line, lineEnd, offset}]`. Grammar: backtick-quoted
  repo-root-relative `` `path` ``, `` `path:12` ``, `` `path:112–118` `` (en-dash **and** hyphen).
  Any extension. **Only inside the evidence marker defined in t1.1b** — never free text.
- **t1.1b THE STRUCTURAL SPLIT — this is what makes the gate possible at all.** The advisor quotes
  corpus text, and corpus text is full of backticked path-like tokens that are **not** the advisor's
  claims. Measured on live rows of [`BACKLOG.md`](BACKLOG.md): **108 path-like backticked tokens, 51
  of which do not resolve from repo root** (`../analysis/LOOP-GOAL-DIRECTION.md`, `wiki/AGENTS.md`,
  `.landing.lock`, …). **A textual scan of the artifact would fire on the first quoted row and the
  artifact would never be written** — AC1.1 unsatisfiable while AC1.3 holds.
  So citations are emitted through **one helper into one recognisable position** — a trailing
  `— evidence: \`path:line\`` on a claim line — and the extractor reads **only** that position.
  Quoted row text is rendered so the extractor provably ignores it.
- **t1.2** `verifyCitations(baseDir, text)` → `{ok, resolved[], unresolved[], truncated}`. A path must
  exist; a line must be within the file; a range needs both ends in range and `line <= lineEnd`.
- **t1.3** Root-confinement before any disk touch (`B22`), and a scan ceiling that **reports
  truncation rather than under-reporting** (`B15`).
- **t1.4** Tests: resolving path; missing path; real path with an out-of-range line; range with one
  end out; en-dash range; non-`.md` extension; **a quoted non-resolving token OUTSIDE the evidence
  marker is ignored** (t1.1b's core case); zero citations → `ok: true` with an empty list.
  ⚠ **`ok: true` on zero citations is correct at the unit and VACUOUS at the gate** — see t3.5, which
  is why the run boundary asserts a **count**, not the flag.

### S2 — Corpus reading (`plugin/tools/lib/advise-corpus.js`)

Reads **five** sources and reports what it could not read. **Shape B**, matching `collectPreflight`
exactly: `{sources, cannotCheck: [{source, reason}], checked: string[]}` plus a frozen
**`ADVISOR_SOURCES = ['BACKLOG.md', 'BUGS.md', 'retrospectives', 'STATE/closure', 'milestone rows']`**
— enumerated here because `t2.6` tests *"each source unreadable in turn"* and an unenumerated list
makes FR7 only as complete as whatever the executor remembers. *(The first draft said "four" and
listed five tasks.)*

- **t2.1 Backlog** — `parseBacklogRows(content, { maxDepth: 4 })`. Filter `inDetails` (preserved
  history, not live work) and `discharged`. Row bodies derived from consecutive `line` values —
  **no second heading walk.**
- **t2.2 Bugs** — **additive change to `walkBugEntries`**: add `line` to each record. Additive so
  `deriveBugCounts` and `readClosureSources` keep working. ⚠ **Do not fork the walker** — that
  module's own docblock says *"writing the walk twice is `B82`'s shape."*
- **t2.3 Retros** — `enumerateRetros(baseDir)`, then read + `parseSections`. **Honour `isStub`: a
  retro carrying `[FILL IN]` is not evidence and must never be cited as a finished unit.**
- **t2.4 State/closure** — read via `resolveClosures` (`closure.js`), **not raw `readState`**, and
  never let a `StateSchemaError` escape. An unreadable state yields `cannotCheck`, never an empty
  set: *"an empty map says 'nothing is closed', which is a result; a null says 'I could not look',
  which is not."*
- **t2.5 Milestone rows** — export **one** reader covering both published formats (`| **E1** |` and
  `` | `M6.E1` | ``) and use it here.
  ⚠ **Re-pointing the two existing private call sites is a SEPARATE task, deliberately.**
  `findEpicStatusRow` feeds `isEpicCloseShip`, so re-pointing it makes `D-E9-5`'s override live again
  across Milestone 6 and **changes when the SHIP retro gate fires**. That is a real behaviour change
  and does not belong inside an advisory Epic by side effect. Land the export here; do the re-point
  under its own bug row, with its own test.
- **t2.6** Tests: each source unreadable in turn → its own `cannotCheck` entry with a reason, and
  **never** an empty result presented as clean. Pattern from `tests/pr-review-findings.test.js`'s
  *"cannot-check is a value, never a silent pass"*, including the double assertion
  `expect(status).toBe('cannot-check'); expect(status).not.toBe('clean')`.
- **t2.7 The depth test runs BOTH directions.** Measured on [`BACKLOG.md`](BACKLOG.md): depth-3 sees
  **43** live rows, depth-4 sees **50** — nine `####` rows invisible at the default. But widening also
  moves headings into the container fold: **two rows present at depth 3 disappear at depth 4**
  (lines 658, 743). Both are genuine section headers here, so widening is safe *on this file* —
  `backlog.js` warns it *"changes which rows they strike"*, and on a project where a real `###` row
  has `####` children the row is silently dropped. Assert both:
  `live(4) ⊇ live(3) \ containers`, and the nine `####` lines are present.

### S3 — The advisor, the artifact, and the gate (`plugin/tools/lib/advise.js` + `plugin/commands/advise.md`)

- **t3.1 Ranking — specified, because the first draft was one line for the Epic's entire product
  logic.** `N = 5`, a stated constant in the module with its reason beside it: the frozen review's
  own headline offers *"Top three moves"*, and five leaves room to be wrong twice without becoming a
  list nobody reads. **Not** a `PROFILE.md` field (NFR3 — a fourth dial is `B75`).
  **Ranking inputs, ordered and each independently citable:** (1) blocked-by — a row whose stated
  gate is unmet ranks below one whose gate is met; (2) trigger-met — a row whose written trigger has
  fired; (3) discharge state from `backlogDischargeStatus` — a row whose work already closed drops
  out entirely; (4) age since filing. **Stable tiebreak: source line number**, so equal-rank rows
  never reorder between runs (NFR1, and `t3.6`'s byte-compare depends on it).
  **The declined pool is every live row not in the top N** — that is what makes `B39`'s
  checked-vs-unchecked distinction real; a curated subset would leave the rest unchecked and
  indistinguishable.
- **t3.2** A **declined** section — every live row not recommended, each with a reason naming which
  ranking input demoted it (`B39`: a checked-and-declined item must be distinguishable from an
  unchecked one).
- **t3.2b FR6 — proposes, never selects — becomes a rendering contract with a test.** *(The first
  draft had no task and no test for FR6 at all; it is a different requirement from FR5, which is
  about writing.)* The artifact carries the *"changes nothing on its own"* status line; the ranked
  list is labelled **recommended**, never *chosen* or *selected*; no code path marks an item decided.
  Tested by asserting the status line is present and the forbidden verbs are absent.
- **t3.3** `renderArtifact(result)` — pure, file-facing, separate from the terminal formatter, per
  `permissions-report.js`. Header copies the frozen review's five elements: title + date, one-line
  subtitle, the **"changes nothing on its own"** status line, **Corpus read** (the natural home for
  `checked` / `cannotCheck`), and the **Citation rule**.
- **t3.4** ⚠ **A producer attribution line — `via /sig:advise` — because the outcome oracle depends on
  it.** The frozen review has none, and this Epic's own DISCUSS had to label its provenance
  *unverified* as a result. Convention exists: `add.js` and `checkpoint.js` both write `via /sig:…`.
- **t3.5 THE RUN-BOUNDARY GATE — asserts a COUNT, not a flag.** Before writing, run
  `verifyCitations` over the rendered artifact. Require **`unresolved.length === 0` AND
  `resolved.length >= recommendations + declined`**. Any failure fails the run and the artifact is
  **not written**.
  ⚠ **`ok === true` alone is vacuous here.** Zero citations yields `ok: true` (correctly, at the
  unit), so an extractor that missed the renderer's grammar would pass an artifact with **nothing
  checked at all**. AC1.2's *"whole output, not sampled"* is only ever as whole as
  `extractCitations` — **extraction recall is the hole, not sampling** — and a count is what
  measures it.
- **t3.6** `writeArtifact` → `{status:'written'|'unchanged'|'skipped', path, reason}`, idempotent by
  byte-compare, refusing with a reason when `.planning/` is absent. Use `atomicWrite` +
  `assertRealInsidePlanning` — ⚠ `permissions-report.js` uses raw `writeFileSync` and is **the
  outlier, not the convention**.
- **t3.7** Artifact name `BACKLOG-REVIEW-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. **Constrained, not chosen:**
  `tools/doc-budgets.json` already exempts exactly that pattern; any other name needs a manifest
  entry or fails `doc-budgets.test.js` above 32 KB. ⚠ It also collides with `backlog.js`'s
  `findSnapshot` glob — harmless here, real on a fresh project — and **`ORPHAN_ENTRY_POINTS` does not
  match it**, so `/sig:docs-sweep` will flag it until `INDEX.md` regenerates. **Decision: the inbound link
  and the index regeneration are ONE-TIME HUMAN EDITS AT SHIP, not command behaviour.** *(The first
  draft wrote this as a command action, which breached AC1.5 twice over — editing `BACKLOG.md` and
  writing `INDEX.md` — in a plan whose own `t3.6` correctly refuses to write anything else.)* The
  command writes its artifact and nothing else, full stop.
- **t3.8** The 23rd command touches **four** count sites: `roster.js`'s walk, `doc-hygiene.js`'s
  `ROSTER_SITES` (a **HARD fail** pinned to CLAUDE.md's `22 slash commands`),
  `plugin/references/command-taxonomy.md`, and `sweep.js`'s frontmatter check.
- **t3.8b The command's own contract.** `plugin/commands/advise.md`: no arguments in the first slice;
  taxonomy group **orientation** (it reports, it does not advance the flow); and it **does not read
  `PROFILE.md` first** — the tier-gating preamble is a *phase*-command rule and this is not a phase
  command, the same posture `/sig:status` and `/sig:doctor` take. Said explicitly because silence
  reads as an omission.
- **t3.9 NFR2/AC1.6** — a static scan asserting no import of or shell-out to anything under `prose`,
  following `tests/plugin-boundary.test.js`'s **reach declaration**: state what the detector reaches
  and what it does not, because *"a detector that reports 'no matches' without declaring its reach is
  `B81`."*

**Placement is fixed, not preferred:** everything ships in `plugin/tools/lib/`. Repo-root `tools/*.js`
is maintainer-only and `tests/plugin-boundary.test.js` treats a payload reference to it as a
violation.

## Risks, and what would falsify the plan

- **The `maxDepth: 3` blindness is the one that ships silently.** t2.7 exists solely for it.
- **A mutation test proves the case you thought of, not coverage.** `tests/environment.test.js`
  records the humbling precedent: *"ALL SIX PASSED THE FIRST SHIPPED VERSION. A full suite, three
  mutation tests, and my own read of the code did not find one of them."* Stated here so VERIFY does
  not read green mutation tests as proof of completeness.
- **The ranking may be the less valuable half.** The frozen review's best content came from
  cross-document contradiction finding, which this plan does not attempt. `M6.E7-REQUIREMENTS.md`
  pre-commits to reporting that honestly rather than rationalising a low calibration score.
