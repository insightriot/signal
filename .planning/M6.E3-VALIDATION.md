## What this validation could not establish

- It does **not** establish that the receipt rule is the right rule. That was settled in
  conversation and pressure-tested by one reader, not by an independent adversarial pass — a direct
  consequence of the declared research trim (`RESEARCH` § *Declared deviation*). **If REVIEW finds
  the rule underspecified, the trim is the first place to look.**
- It does **not** establish that a receipt is always *producible* for a true case-(a) instance. If
  a bug was fixed with no CHANGELOG entry and no traceable commit, the check correctly stays silent
  and the stale row survives. That is a **known, accepted hole**, and `AC7.1`'s disclosure must not
  imply otherwise.
- Test **counts** here are planned, not measured. The real denominators land in VERIFICATION.
