## The three I am least sure of — flagged rather than buried

- **#16 `codebase-mapper` → cut.** The only `cut` in the set. Its description (*"explores codebase
  and writes structured analysis documents"*) and `codebase-researcher`'s (*"analyzes existing
  codebase for patterns, conventions, reusable assets"*) are close enough that keeping both means
  choosing between them at every dispatch. **If you would rather keep it, the honest verdict is
  `dormant`, not `wire`** — nothing has ever needed the second one.
- **#11 `research-synthesizer`.** Only earns its place if #8–#10 land. It is listed `wire` on the
  condition that they do; if the research trio is cut back, this becomes `cut`.
- **#18 `roadmapper`.** `init.md` already dispatches, so the cost is low — but Signal's own roadmap
  has always been written by hand, so this wires a capability with no evidence anyone wants it.
