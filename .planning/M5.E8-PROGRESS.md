**Result: 91 of 407 directive lines (22.4%) are trace-measurable; 316 (77.6%) are not.**
(lib-call 74 · artifact-write 17)

**Three things worth carrying forward:**

1. **The number is not the planned one.** Research estimated 22/202 = 10.9%. The measured
   figure is 91/407 = 22.4%. Reconciled in the same commit across `CONTEXT.md`, `M5.E8-PLAN.md`
   and a superseded-by-measurement block in `M5.E8-RESEARCH.md` (estimate left verbatim — the
   plan was built on it, and deleting it would hide that).

2. **A load-bearing research claim was falsified.** *"`execute.md`, `verify.md`, `review.md`
   and `calibrate.md` name zero library calls, so the phases doing the most work are the least
   measurable."* They name **4 / 3 / 3 / 0** — including `transitionPhase`, this Epic's own
   canary. The canary set is **not** confined to low-risk phases.

3. **The rule was corrected twice by its own fixture, both times toward a smaller/honest number.**
   - The first implementation dropped leading-clause directives ("Before any Workflow step,
     call X") — the corpus's most common shape, and the shape of the canary instruction itself.
   - Stage B2 originally accepted a write verb and an artifact merely *co-occurring on a line*.
     On a corpus with 600–950 character lines that is close to meaningless: a hand-audit of all
     25 hits found **8 false positives**, every one the noun *"record"* or the phrase
     *"write-only file"* sitting 110–325 characters from an unrelated artifact name. Now the
     verb must precede its artifact within 40 chars with no sentence boundary between.
     This correction **lowered** the measurable count (99 → 91) — the direction that matters,
     since the numerator is a claim about how much Signal can see.

**Out-of-slice change, deliberate and surgical:** `ADHERENCE-LOG.md` added to
`APPEND_LOG_BASENAMES` in `migrate-memory.js`. Without it a permanently-growing measurement
history classifies as `other` and becomes a bloat-relocation candidate — i.e. the doc-runtime
would eventually do to this log exactly what FR4 forbids. Registered at file-creation time
rather than in S4, because the misclassification window opens the moment the file exists.

**Carry-forward to SHIP:** AC5.2 also requires the fraction in the Epic's **retrospective**.
Not yet written — retro lands at SHIP.
