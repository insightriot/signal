The first-draft goal — *"a plan carries what the builder needs, and its own numbers hold up"* —
covered S1 and S3 and **did not cover S2 at all**. It had dropped the **reads** half of the Epic's
own defining phrase, *"what PLAN reads and writes"*.

**The slice was not the problem; the sentence was.** `commands/plan.md` Step 1b is PLAN reading the
inbox, and it is a required step. Goal widened; correction left visible in the PLAN.

**Now:** S1 → *numbers hold up*. S2 → *reads a clean inbox*. S3 → *carries what the builder needs*.
3 of 3 aligned.

### 3. Testability — **PASS, with one criterion honestly marked unenforceable**

Every criterion has a test **except `AC1.2c`**, which states that nothing fails if the plan-checker
prompt ignores the worklist. That is `B75`'s shape and is deliberate.

**It is verified as *documented*, not as *enforced*** — the test asserts the disclosure exists at
the point of use. Claiming a test for the behaviour itself would be the exact false-coverage claim
`analysis/CLAIM-INTEGRITY-ANALYSIS.md` is about.
