Older than M6.E16 and exposure to git itself, not to Signal alone: a `.git/config` that sets `core.fsmonitor`, `core.hooksPath` or `remote.<name>.uploadpack` runs commands at Signal's `git status`, `git tag` and `git fetch` (ran), as it would at the user's own. A clone never produces such a config; an unpacked tarball or a copied folder does. Possible: add `core.fsmonitor=false` and `core.hooksPath=/dev/null` to `gitEnv()` (Signal makes no commits, so it needs no hooks) after checking no Signal call relies on either.
