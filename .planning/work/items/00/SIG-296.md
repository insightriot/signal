Found in M6.E16 S10 (t10.1). `runConfirmCloses` in `plugin/tools/lib/close-confirm.js` evicts a unit's narrative from `STATE.md` in the run that confirms its close. If that eviction is refused (the unit is held, its retrospective is missing or lossy, its record does not read) or throws, the confirmation stands and the refusal is reported — but no later sweep, SHIP or checkpoint tries again, because the unit is never newly confirmed a second time and `/sig:checkpoint` evicts only `current_epic`. `ship.md` §6.9 says to call `evictEpicNarrative` by hand.

Options, none chosen: (a) the sweep reports every closed unit that still has a narrative section in `STATE.md`; (b) `/sig:checkpoint` evicts every closed unit with a narrative, not only the current one.
