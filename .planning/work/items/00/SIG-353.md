Found during M6.E18 S4 (2026-10-20). `parent-run.js` prints a 7-character sha, and M6.E18 S4 made `checkFixRounds` resolve the **Parent run:** commit with `git rev-parse --verify <sha>^{commit}`. In a repository large enough for a 7-character prefix to be ambiguous, rev-parse fails and the round is refused as "does not resolve" though the commit exists. `commitsStartingWith` in `plugin/tools/lib/git-objects.js` can tell ambiguous from absent; or have parent-run.js print a longer sha.
