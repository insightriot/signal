stringifyFrontmatter(data, body) builds `---\n${yaml}\n---\n${body}` with no check, so a caller that passes no body writes the word undefined at the top of the STATE.md body. Seen in a real project: "undefined# Project State" appeared on 2026-09-14, after a session wrote STATE by hand because transitionPhase refused, and doubled to "undefinedundefined" on the next hand write. It stayed until a person noticed it six weeks later. Fix: refuse a body that is not a string, with a test.
