**`diffRequirementCoverage`'s denominator silently drops a real requirement on 21 of 22 REQUIREMENTS artifacts in this repo — so `missing: []` can report clean over a requirement it never counted.** Found 2026-09-05 **by using the tool**, during `M6.E7` VERIFY: the derived denominator came back **15** against a hand-derived **16**, and the one it lost was **`FR1`**. ⚠ **This is NOT a naive prefix collision, and reading the source is what said so.** `groupOf` deliberately normalizes `AC{N}` to the `FR-{N}` keyspace, and `dropGroupLabels`'s own docblock states the intent: *"`FR-16` sitting above `AC-16.1`…`AC-16.11` is the name of a group whose criteria are the things a VERIFICATION covers … counting it as its own requirement inflates every denominator by one."* That is correct **for the convention `M5.E10` uses**, where `AC-16.x` really are `FR-16`'s children. **It is wrong for the convention almost every other Epic uses**, where `FR1…FR7` are independent requirements and `AC1.1…AC1.6` are the *unit's* acceptance criteria numbered `1.x` — not children of `FR1`. Under the shared-group rule `AC1.1` marks group `FR-1` as having children, and `FR1` is dropped as a heading. Minimal repro: `dropGroupLabels(['FR1','FR2'])` keeps both; `dropGroupLabels(['FR1','FR2','AC1.1'])` returns `['FR2','AC1.1']`. **Measured blast radius: 21 of 22 `*-REQUIREMENTS.md` artifacts lose at least one id.** `M5.E16` loses **all six** of its FRs, leaving a denominator of ACs only; `M5.E9` loses eight FRs plus `AC1` and `AC2`; `M6.E4` and `M6.E5` lose their `AC{N}` group rows. ⚠ **The consequence is a FALSE CLEAN in the check built to stop unfalsifiable coverage claims.** `missing` is computed as `denominator.filter(not verified)`, so a requirement absent from the denominator **cannot** be reported missing — a unit that never verified `FR1` still gets `missing: []` and a green exit criterion. `M5.E10` built this to stop *"all criteria verified"* with no stated total; the total it states is wrong on 95% of this repo's own units. **Not fixed inside `M6.E7`** — different module, different Epic, and a fix has to choose between the two conventions or detect which one an artifact uses, which is a design call and not a patch. Fix lane, its own PR, and the test must assert the `M5.E10` shape still collapses correctly **and** the `M6.E7` shape stops dropping `FR1` — one without the other just moves the bug.
