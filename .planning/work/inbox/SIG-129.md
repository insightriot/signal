## Whole-population deny assertion — the shape B81 needs

**Status:** needs-triage

**Approach for `B81`, borrowed from `vercel-labs/eve-software-factory-template` (MIT).** Its eval harness enumerates the whole write-tool population once, in `evals/helpers.ts`, and every read-only eval asserts `notCalledTool` across **all of it** rather than naming the one tool a bad run might reach for. Its own comment states the property: *"a new write tool added to the extension is automatically forbidden in every read-only eval until someone allows it deliberately."*

That is deny-by-default over an enumerated population, and it is exactly the shape `B81` needs. `guard-callers.test.js` today governs **1 of ~25 CLI flags** in `tools/` and its "population is not empty" assertion cannot distinguish that from governing all of them — so the fix is not "detect more flags", it is "assert over the declared population and fail when a member is ungoverned."

Two details worth copying with it: the population list is **kept next to the thing it mirrors** with an explicit sync note naming the source file, and the *exceptions are named individually* with a reason (`read_factory_brain` and `read_artifact` are deliberately absent from the deny list, and the comment says why). An exception with a written reason is auditable; an omission is not.

Reference: `evals/helpers.ts` in that repo. Not a straight port — Signal has no tool-call event stream to assert against — but the population-assertion shape transfers to `tools/` flags directly.
