| B123 | `needs-triage` | P3 | **`/sig:advise`'s `today` parameter is not validated before it becomes the artifact's filename.** `runAdvise` takes `today` from its caller and interpolates it into `BACKLOG-REVIEW-${stamp}.md`, which `writeArtifact` then passes to `assertRealInsidePlanning`. The pass-3 security audit probed it read-only and reports that traversals out of `.planning/` are **refused** by that guard, but that a stamp of `/../archive/x` is **allowed** and would write the advisory into `.planning/archive/` instead of the planning root. ⚠ **Filed as `needs-triage` rather than `confirmed` because the author's own re-probe was inconclusive** — it refused every stamp including the valid one, which means the probe did not reproduce the auditor's setup rather than that the finding is wrong. Caller-supplied and outside the command's stated threat model (the only caller is `commands/advise.md`), so it is a hardening item, not a live defect. Fix: validate `stamp` against `^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}$` in `runAdvise` and refuse otherwise. |
