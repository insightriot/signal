**What this ranking is built on, stated so it can be attacked.** Four criteria, all auditable, none
of them frequency:

1. **Independent-document count** — how many separate documents raise it. *Not* a frequency count:

   four dates, not four occurrences of one event.
2. **Recorded recency** — dates that exist in the file, per S1.t2 §5's per-file reality.
3. **Demonstrated cost** — did it cost something auditable (a shipped defect, a live incident).
4. **Ledger coverage** — a verified 0-hit grep, not an impression.

net-new retro items, **15 come from M5-era retros**, 10 from M4.5-era, 1 from v0.1.6 — and **not one
M5-era item has a BACKLOG home**. The corpus's newest signal is its least-tracked, which is the
reverse of what a healthy promotion pipeline would look like.
