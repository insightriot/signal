## 1. Eight-dimension validation

### D1 — Goal alignment · **PASS**
Goal: a ratifiable, evidence-grounded v2 roadmap whose `build` items land enforceably.
FR1/FR2 → evidence · FR3/FR7 → judgment · FR4 → deliverable · FR5 → enforcement · FR6 → prevents
the next reader re-deriving the wrong plan. No task fails to trace.

*Weakest link:* **S4.t10 (correct the record)** serves the goal only indirectly. Retained because
three falsified claims sit in load-bearing docs — C3 in particular means Signal has been planning
to port a **syntax** (`<HARD-GATE>`) instead of the **mechanism** (SDD's five-round breaker). That
is worth a task on its own.

### D2 — Completeness · **PASS**
All seven FRs are covered: FR1→S1 · FR2→S2 · FR3→S3.t8 · FR4→S4.t9 · FR5→S4.t11 · FR6→S4.t10 ·
FR7→S3.t7. P1–P8 are bound to S4.t9. AC3.2's five-item no-silent-drops floor is explicit in t8.

### D3 — Dependency correctness · **FAIL → FIXED**
**Defect found: S4.t10 carried a false dependency.** It was sequenced after S4.t9, but the
corrections it makes (C3/C4/C5 from supply verification; C6 from PLAN research) depend on **S2
only** — not on the demand register, the counterfactual, or the disposition table. Sequencing it
behind t9 delayed the highest-confidence, lowest-dependency output in the Epic behind the
lowest-confidence one.

**Fix applied:** t10 is unblocked as soon as S2 completes and moves to Wave 2, running parallel to
S3. It can land before the roadmap exists — the record is wrong *today*.

*Kept deliberately:* S2.t4 → t5/t6 is a **partial** dependency (Layer-3 conclusions only), and the
plan says so rather than pretending the three verifies are strictly serial.

### D4 — Testability (→ verifiability) · **PASS with a caveat**
Every AC has a concrete check (§2). Roughly half are **mechanical** (grep for citations, count
verbs vs. candidates, assert the five seed items by name, suite green) and half are **judgment**
(is a theme cluster right? is a disposition rationale sound?).

**Honest caveat:** the mechanical checks verify that each candidate *got* a verb — they cannot
verify the verb was *right*. **Nothing in this plan can.** The only defense is the adversarial
REVIEW (AC7.4), and that is where this Epic fails if it fails. Stated rather than papered over.

### D5 — Scope discipline · **PASS with a flagged growth**
FR6 and FR7 did not exist at DISCUSS; both emerged from research. That is **scope growth**, and
naming it is the point:
- **FR6 (S)** — justified: three falsified claims in load-bearing docs.
- **FR7 (M)** — justified: the strongest mitigation available for a High, structural risk.

Net additive ≈ one slice's worth. Accepted deliberately, not absorbed silently.
Out-of-scope list is explicit and holds the line on the tempting adjacents (backfilling stubs,
building the `analysis/` staleness check, changing the tier system).

### D6 — Context feasibility · **FAIL → FIXED**
**Defect found: S2.t6 (compound-engineering) does not fit one agent context.** It requires
re-mapping 758 files / 32 `ce-*` skills / 27 agents nested per-skill / 17 personas, *plus*
evaluating three new capabilities, *plus* re-deriving all four of Signal's wanted bullets. That is
an L task that reads as two.

**Fix applied:** split into
- **t6a — re-map the architecture** (agent relocation, persona-dispatch rebuild, the four wanted
  bullets re-derived)
- **t6b — evaluate the three new capabilities** (cross-model adversarial review, `ce-retune`, `ce-pov`)

t6b is the higher-value half and was at risk of being crowded out by t6a's mechanical mapping.

All other tasks are single-context: S1.t1 is ~1,510 words; S1.t2 is capped at headings + Status
lines; S2.t4/t5 are S/M against small repos.

### D7 — Risk coverage · **PASS**
Six risks carried with mitigations and honest residuals. Two are **not** claimed as solved:
- **Confirmation bias — High, structural.** The audit is run by the pair that built the thing,
  over a corpus they wrote, which is silent about work never attempted. Mitigated four ways
  (S1∥S2 separation, mandatory counterfactual, hypothesis framing, adversarial REVIEW) and
  **eliminated by none.**
- **Shelf-ware — Medium.** AC5.6 is a real mitigation (`BACKLOG.md` is live and hygiene-touched),
  but nothing forces the roadmap itself to be consulted.

The counterfactual pass (S3.t7) is sequenced **before** dispositioning, which is what makes it a
guard rather than a rationalization.

### D8 — Vertical slicing · **PASS, with the doc-Epic adaptation stated**
Each slice ships a durable, independently useful artifact section:
- **S1** — the demand register stands alone as first-ever harvest of the retro corpus
- **S2** — the supply menu stands alone; **it already corrects the record**
- **S3** — the disposition table is the decision
- **S4** — the deliverable + enforcement

Not horizontal layers. **S1∥S2 is required for integrity (AC2.4), not merely allowed** — a supply
verifier that already knows what the demand register wants will find it.
