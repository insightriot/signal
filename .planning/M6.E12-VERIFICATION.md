## What this could not establish

- **Whether the priorities are good.** Tests check the contract (counts, fields, citations, covered
  rows), never the judgment. The only evidence is use: one real run, one pick of a proposed priority
  — after a clarifying question that exposed the missing dependency check. The outcome measure
  (`D-M6E12-12`) needs two more runs.
- **Evidence lines say what the `why` claims.** Priority `evidence` is checked for existence only;
  only covered rows and bugs are re-read for content. A fluent `why` citing an unrelated line passes.
- **The coverage tool's own blind spots.** `diffRequirementCoverage` undercounts its denominator on
  most artifacts here (`B116`) and counts a mention as verified (`B126`) — the very bugs priority 2
  would fix. The 26-of-26 above was counted by hand for that reason.
- **`/sig:drive`'s new step 0a has never run.** It is prose, checked by doc-contract tests; no
  `/sig:drive` run has exercised the priorities flow. Same gap as `SIG-163`.
- **Other projects.** The digest ran on this repository and on `examples/sandbox/` only. No production
  project was used (standing rule); other corpus shapes are untested.
- **The `AskUserQuestion` five-option path.** Only the 3-option picker was exercised for real; the
  numbered-prompt path for five priorities is prose, untested.
