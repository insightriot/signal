## Phase goal

Make `/sig:ship` work for a project that has no Epics, and make the phase ledger an
honest append-only log that trims instead of silently deleting itself.

docs that describe it — and each is independently shippable.

## Deliberate omissions

- **No 4-agent research pass.** Reasoned and flagged for override in `M5.E9-RESEARCH.md` §
  Method — not skipped silently.
- **The inbox drain (Step 1b) was skipped, not batch-deferred.** 47 candidates are live, but
  M5.E7 already dispositioned 45 of them into `M5.E7-DISPOSITIONS.md` **without stamping the
  inbox**. Stamping *"Deferred (M5.E9 drain)"* over entries already verbed `build` or
  `abandon` would write **wrong** dispositions into the idea database to satisfy a checklist.
  **The reconciliation gap is captured as its own finding** rather than papered over here.
- **No new dependencies, no new commands.** Every FR lands in an existing module.
