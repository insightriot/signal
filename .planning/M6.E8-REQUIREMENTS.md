# `M6.E8` — The advisor's unused corpus

`/sig:advise` reads five sources and ranks on one. This Epic makes two of the other four count,
deletes one that never will, and keeps the last with the reason written down.

Decisions: [`DECISIONS.md`](DECISIONS.md) § *2026-09-07 — M6.E8 DISCUSS* (`D-M6E8-1` … `D-M6E8-6`).
Backlog row: [`BACKLOG.md`](BACKLOG.md), *"`/sig:advise` ranks on the backlog alone, while reading
five sources"*, filed 2026-09-05 from `M6.E7` REVIEW.

**Two of the row's own proposals do not survive contact with that table**, and the Epic is shaped

## Functional requirements

**FR1 — A bug-discharge input, read from the heading only.** A live row whose **heading** says it
fixes or closes a bug that `BUGS.md` still records as `confirmed` ranks above a row that does not.
Vocabulary is measured and declared before it is chosen, and it fires on **zero** rows today — the
basis on which `shelved` already ships.

**FR2 — The blocked vocabulary widens to catch the phrasing already in the file.** `BLOCKED_RE` gains
*"entry price for"* (and any sibling phrase the vocabulary measurement turns up). This is where the
Epic's live effect comes from: four rows currently reading `blocked: false` are gated on `B75` and
should read `blocked: true`.

**FR3 — A closed-Epic drop input, on `leadingId` and nothing looser.** A live row whose own leading
Epic ID resolves to `closed` via `resolveClosures` drops out of the ranking with a reason, the way
discharged rows already do. The loose "heading mentions a closed unit" predicate is **prohibited**,
not merely unused.

**FR4 — A fold vocabulary, with `KEPT` as an override evaluated first.** A row whose heading says its
work moved elsewhere (`FOLDED INTO`, `absorbed into`, and whatever else the measurement finds) drops
out — **unless** the heading also says `KEPT`, which preserves it as ranked live work. Two
vocabularies, opposite effects, same heading position.

**FR5 — The retrospective read is removed.** `ADVISOR_SOURCES` goes from five entries to four;
`readCorpus` stops enumerating and parsing retrospectives; the artifact's **Corpus read** section
stops naming them. 32 files per run stop being parsed for headings nothing consults.

**FR6 — The milestone read stays, and its source says why.** It is cheap and it is the natural home
for a future *"already sequenced into an open Epic"* input. Kept **with a written reason** is a
different state from kept by inertia, and the difference has to be legible in the source.

**FR7 — The artifact tells the truth about which sources the ranking used.** The *"Consulted by the
ranking: `BACKLOG.md` only"* line — added by `M6.E7` REVIEW because the old wording let a reader
infer more — is updated to name the sources actually consulted after FR1–FR4, and no others.

## Non-functional requirements

**NFR1 — The read-only contract is unchanged.** `runAdvise` still writes exactly one artifact and
`writeArtifact` still refuses everything else. No backlog row is struck, nothing reaches
`DECISION-QUEUE.md`, `INDEX.md` and `BACKLOG.md` remain one-time human SHIP edits.

**NFR2 — No second definition of "closed" or "not live."** Closure comes from `resolveClosures`,
discharge from `backlogDischargeStatus`, bug status from the `BUGS.md` reader already in
`readCorpus`. A local re-implementation of any of them is the thing `M5.E19` spent a slice removing.

**NFR3 — `HELD_OPEN_RE` is not widened.** `backlogDischargeStatus` reads it to mean *"declared open on
purpose, do not flag as stale"*; changing it would alter a shipped check as a side effect.

**NFR4 — No new `PROFILE.md` field.** A fourth dial documented end to end and read by nothing is
`B75`'s shape.

**NFR5 — The citation gate and the stale-read guard still hold.** `verifyCitations` still asserts
`unresolved.length === 0` **and** a citation count ≥ recommended + declined; `findStaleRowCitations`
still refuses to write when a cited line no longer carries its row. Neither is relaxed to accommodate
a new reason string.

**NFR6 — Every vocabulary is measured on the real file before it is chosen**, and the count is
recorded in the source next to the pattern — including a count of **zero**, declared rather than
implied (`backlog.js`'s existing standard).

## Blast radius — what this touches that it does not change

- **`plugin/tools/lib/advise.js` / `advise-corpus.js`** — the changed code. `runAdvise` has one
  caller (`commands/advise.md`).
- **`BACKLOG-REVIEW-2026-09-06.md`, already published and linked from `BACKLOG.md`.** It is a dated
  snapshot and is **not** regenerated. A run after this Epic writes a *new* dated file; the old one
  stands as the record of what the old ranking said.
- **`declaresNotLiveWork` and `BLOCKED_RE` are shared surface.** `declaresNotLiveWork` is read by the
  advisor only; `BLOCKED_RE` is local to `advise.js`. `HELD_OPEN_RE` and `backlogDischargeStatus` are
  read elsewhere and are **not** touched (NFR3).
- **`resolveClosures` and the `BUGS.md` reader gain a caller**, not a change.
- **Downstream consumers:** none. Nothing parses the advisory artifact; it is read by a person.
- **`tools/doc-budgets.json`** already exempts the `BACKLOG-REVIEW-*` pattern; no manifest change.

## Outcome

**How we will know it worked, measured on this repository's own backlog, by diffing the next
advisory against `BACKLOG-REVIEW-2026-09-06.md`:**

1. **Three rows move** from ranked-live into the declined pool with a reason naming the fold input
   (lines 808, 1802, 1947), and **two are preserved** by the `KEPT` override (1872, 1879).
2. **Four rows** currently reading `blocked: false` while gated on `B75` demote below the ungated
   rows — visible as a changed decline reason on each.
3. **The Corpus read section names four sources, not five**, and the *"Consulted by the ranking"*
   line names more than `BACKLOG.md`.
4. **The recommended five change composition** as a consequence of 1 and 2 — the point of the Epic is
   that the ranking moves, and a ranking that does not move means the inputs did not fire.

All four are derivable from the artifact itself, which is the property `M6.E7` VERIFY failed to
exercise on its own numbers.

⚠ **What this outcome does NOT measure: whether the resulting recommendation is *better*.** Ranking
quality has no oracle here — `LOOP-GOAL-DIRECTION.md` measured the best honest selection rule over
these exact rows at 77% precision / ~37% recall and recommended against automatic selection. This
Epic makes the inputs fire correctly and does not claim the output is wiser. Stated now rather than
discovered at VERIFY.
