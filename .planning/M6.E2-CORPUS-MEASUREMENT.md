# M6.E2 — corpus measurement: does any of this reach a project that is not this one?

**Run 2026-08-18, read-only, via `tools/measure-published-facts.js ~/dev-biz`.** Reproduce with that
command. Labels are pinned by hash (`references/eval-corpus.md`); no project name appears here.

## Why this ran before scope was locked

The Epic was pitched on **five instances found in five files of this repository**. Signal's own
`.planning/` is the **minority** shape — `M5.E16` measured its two headline checks reaching 2 of 13
projects, and `B82` lived only where unit names are not Epic IDs. A check measured only here is a
check whose reach is unknown, and this repo's standard is that the reach *is* the finding
(`M5.E10`: *"seven checks shipped is not the honest summary — the reach is"*).

## The result

12 local projects carry a `.planning/`. **`eval-project-?` is this repository** — it is unlabelled
because its directory name is not in the pinned map, and it is listed separately below because
including Signal in its own reach count is how a Signal-only convention reads as general.

| Published fact | Reach incl. Signal | Reach **excluding** Signal |
|---|---|---|
| `BUGS.md` present | 4 / 12 | **3 / 11** |
| …carrying a published tally | 1 / 12 | **0 / 11** |
| `CHANGELOG.md` present | 5 / 12 | 4 / 11 |
| …carrying an `[Unreleased]` heading | 4 / 12 | **4 / 11** |
| Milestone file with Epic-status rows | 1 / 12 | **0 / 11** |

## What this does to the pitch — three of five instances are Signal-only

**Corrected here rather than tidied away, because the correction is the useful part.**

- **The `BUGS.md` tally check reaches exactly one project: this one.** Three other projects have a
  `BUGS.md`; **none** publishes a tally. A registered drift check for it would evaluate 1 of 12 and
  report nothing about the other 11.
- **The milestone Epic-status check is the same** — 1 of 12, and that 1 is Signal.
- **`facts.md` headline figures (`B56`) are a Signal artifact**, not a Signal-user artifact.

**Two instances survive with real reach:**

- **`[Unreleased]` vs. the released version — 4 of 11 projects excluding Signal.** This is the one
  general check in the set, and doc-hygiene currently skips `[Unreleased]` by design, so it is aimed
  away from the defect.
- **`/sig:add --bug` writing below a footer is a command defect, not a convention defect.**
  `captureToBugs` inserts with `insertAtEnd` on the stated belief that `BUGS.md` has no footer. That
  belief is wrong for *any* `BUGS.md` carrying a trailing footer, whether or not the footer is a
  tally. It reaches the 3 non-Signal projects that have a `BUGS.md` the moment one grows a footer,
  and it reaches this one today.

## What this measurement cannot establish

- **It counts shapes, not intentions.** A project without a tally today may want one; a check with
  0/11 reach now is not proof the convention is worthless, only that nothing there publishes it yet.
- **The corpus is 12 local projects, all one maintainer's.** It is not a sample of Signal's users.
- **`[Unreleased]` presence is not the defect.** The defect is a *dated* `[Unreleased]` heading
  contradicting a released version; this probe detects the heading, not the contradiction. The 4/11
  figure is an upper bound on reach, not a count of affected projects.
- Nothing here measures the fifth instance (`B56` / `facts.md`) beyond noting it is Signal-local.
