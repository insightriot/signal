## 2. ⚠ A near-miss inside this very phase, recorded because it is the Epic's own subject

**The first pass of the AC-verification script checked 20 criteria and printed 20 PASS.** It
omitted **AC2.2** — the only failing one. The output read as a clean sweep and would have been
reported as one.

Nothing was wrong with any individual check. The defect was that the *set* of checks was
assembled from memory of what had been built rather than derived from the enumeration in
`M5.E13-VALIDATION.md`. **That is `B50` exactly** — *a completeness claim written from the shape
of the work rather than from the artifact* — occurring inside the verification step of the Epic
that filed `B50`, one turn after PLAN corrected its own AC count for the same reason.

Caught only because the count was cross-checked against VALIDATION's stated 21. **A verification
that enumerates its own scope cannot detect a criterion it never listed** — the check must be
driven *from* the requirements artifact, not written alongside it. Logged as a REVIEW input; the
mechanism belongs to **M5.E10**, whose subject this is.

**Sighting count for `B50` this Epic: 3.** (1) M5.E8's retro mechanism covering 1 of 3 instances,
caught at DISCUSS. (2) PLAN's own AC count, caught before publishing. (3) this. All three caught,
none by a mechanism — by someone re-deriving a number that was already written down.

## 4. AC2.2 — the failure, stated plainly

**Criterion:** *"`listDrainCandidatesWithRecovery` returns only genuinely undecided candidates; the
45 dispositioned ones do not reappear. Count before and after is recorded."*

**Measured:** before **48**, after **48**, dispositioned **0**.

**Why nothing was stamped.** `B46`'s premise does not survive measurement. Its claim is that
M5.E7's dispositions are verdicts on inbox entries that were never written back. They are not:
`M5.E7-DISPOSITIONS.md`'s population is the **v2 feature-port candidates** (§3 is explicitly
*"`MILESTONE-5.md` § 'Candidate v2 feature-port scope' — 23 items"*), and **0 of the 48 inbox
candidates correspond to any disposition row**. All five of the file's references to the inbox
cite it as *evidence of demand*, never as a target. Stamping would have written verbs from one
population onto a different one — the precise hazard M5.E9 named when it refused a blanket defer.

**Disposition:** work stopped, not forced. `B46` re-triaged in `BUGS.md` with the measurement.
The requirements' own *"Assumptions to validate during EXECUTE"* §2 anticipated a mapping that
might not be 1:1 and instructed: *stamp only what maps unambiguously and report the remainder.*
Nothing maps unambiguously, so nothing was stamped — this is the instruction being followed, not
overridden.

**Residual, still open:** 48 candidates genuinely do resurface at every drain. That cost is real;
its cause is not un-stamped dispositions. Choosing the real remedy is a scope decision, deferred.

**This is loop-back option C** (accept and document) under the phase gate — chosen because the
criterion is not *failed*, it is **inapplicable**: looping back to EXECUTE would mean implementing
a correction the evidence says is wrong.
