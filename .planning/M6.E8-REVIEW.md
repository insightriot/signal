`test-engineer`, by **mutating the backlog** rather than reading the tests). A single ordinary

That is `B120`'s shape — a check that fires when a ledger is merely *used* — and **this Epic's own

same. The test file's header meanwhile asserted *"THIS IS NOT `B120`'s SHAPE"*, and the preceding
commit had **doubled down**, codifying *"bump all seven constants at SHIP"* — a procedure for living
with the defect.

beside the pattern that nothing verified — `NFR6`'s target), then asserted (fires on every edit).
Neither is right, because *a population is not a measurement of a vocabulary*. Removed, with its
absence now asserted. **Measured after: an ordinary row turns 0 red, and the SHIP strike turns
exactly 1** — restoring the header's original one-constant claim, this time from a simulation.
