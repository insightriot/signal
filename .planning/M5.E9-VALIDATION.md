### F1 — Completeness gap found and closed

**AC8.3** (the blind-spot test is *named and commented as a coverage-gap guard*, stating why
Signal-on-Signal cannot reach this path) had no owning task — it would have shipped as an
unlabelled test, which is the failure it exists to prevent: a guard nobody recognizes as a
guard gets deleted in the next cleanup. **Folded into S1.t1** as an explicit deliverable.

### F2 — Slice 2 is at the edge of one agent context

Seven tasks touching `state.js`, `evict.js`, `retrospective.js`, `resume.js`,
`references/state-schema.md` and two test files. It is the largest unit here and the plan's
most likely overrun.

**Named split point, decided now rather than mid-EXECUTE:** if S2 grows, split at
**S2.t1–t3 (the log core) | S2.t4–t6 (the two trim paths)**, keeping S2.t7's doc work with
whichever half lands last. **What must not be split:** the dedupe removal (S2.t2) from the
trim paths — R1/R2 proved that combination ships a regression. The split is along a seam
that preserves that pairing; any other cut does not.
