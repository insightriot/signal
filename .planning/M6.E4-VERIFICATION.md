## What this could not establish

**1. `AC3.1b` is verified only as an instruction, never as a behaviour — and this is the weakest
claim in the report.** The criterion is *"an executor dispatched on a task whose research names an
exemplar follows it rather than re-deriving the pattern."* What is actually asserted is that
`executor.md` **tells** it to. **No executor agent was dispatched during this Epic** — this session's
harness is configured not to spawn subagents, so every slice was executed inline. The behavioural
half of `FR3.1` is therefore **unverified**, not verified-and-passing. It needs one real
`/sig:execute` dispatch on a task carrying an exemplar reference.

**2. `AC1.2c` is enforced by nothing, by design.** Nothing fails if `plan-checker.md` ignores the
worklist, and `detectQuantitativeTasks` cannot observe whether its output was addressed. The test
asserts the **disclosure** exists. It is `B75`'s open shape at a smaller radius and it ships open.

**3. The detector compares tokens; it cannot judge satisfaction.** A plan naming a formula and a
criterion that are *plausibly* related but numerically incompatible is **flagged, not judged** — the
arithmetic stays with the reviewer. This is `M5.E10`'s `AC0.1` limit inherited deliberately, not an
oversight.

**4. Reach is 10 of 13, and the 3 are named.** 1 project has no PLAN artifact; 2 build their plans
entirely from `##` headings. Those 3 render `cannot-check` with a reason. **A `##` fallback was
refused on measurement** — their h2 headings are 8/12 and 10/12 *section* vocabulary, so parsing
them would flag `## Phase goal` as a task.

**5. The corpus flag rate is measured, the reviewer's actual burden is not.** 9.3% corpus-wide and
20.9% here says how many tasks get flagged. It does **not** establish that a reviewer will address
them — which is limit 2 restated in numbers, and the thing to watch after a few real plans.

**6. `M4.5.E9` S1.t11 is a copied fixture, not a live archive read.** Deliberate: `/sig:archive`
exists to move that file. The cost is that a future edit to the archived plan will not be reflected
here — acceptable, since the fixture pins a historical defect that will not change.
