# Ecosystem — capture scratchpad

Competitors, adjacent products, and relevant tech worth keeping an eye on. Free-form
capture, not a tracked artifact: nothing here gates a phase, and no command reads it.

## How to add an item

Drop a name or URL under **Entries** — or just say *"add X to ecosystem.md"* — and
Claude fills in the rest: fetch the site, one search pass for funding/positioning/news,
then write the entry using the template below. Keep each entry short; the point is
recall, not coverage. Label vendor marketing claims as vendor claims.

```markdown
### Name — url
- **What it is:** one line
- **Category:** competitor | adjacent | pattern source
- **Relation to Signal:** one line — why it's here
- **Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
```

## Entries

### Outset — https://outset.ai/

- **What it is:** An AI-moderated customer research platform. Its agents run the
  interviews themselves — video, voice, or text, 40+ languages — probing dynamically
  off what the participant says and does, then synthesize hundreds of those interviews
  into themes and quotes. Customers include Microsoft, Nestlé, HubSpot, Glassdoor, Away,
  and Uber. $51M raised: $17M Series A (Jun 2025), $30M Series B (Dec 2025, led by
  Radical Ventures with M12, Y Combinator, and 8VC). Their claimed gains over human-led
  research — 8× faster, 81% cheaper, 10× reach — are the company's own numbers, not
  independently measured.
- **Category:** adjacent
- **Relation to Signal:** Same mechanism, different end. Both point adaptive AI
  questioning plus synthesis at a human to extract something a fixed form couldn't —
  Outset at *end customers* to produce market insight, Signal at the *builder*
  (`/sig:calibrate`'s 5 diagnostic questions, `/sig:discuss`'s adaptive questioning) to
  calibrate how much rigor the work needs. Their whole business rests on the premise
  that a scripted interview yields garbage and the questioning has to adapt — which is
  outside commercial evidence for the bet `/sig:calibrate` makes. Watch the drift: the
  Series B repositioned them from "AI-moderated research" to "AI-native customer
  experience management," which moves them further from Signal's territory, not closer.
- **Captured:** 2026-08-09
